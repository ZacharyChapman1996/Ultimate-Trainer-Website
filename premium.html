UltimateTrainer Premium is the planned optional subscription for players who want access to additional capabilities as the platform develops. The goal is to build on the core UltimateTrainer experience while keeping the standard experience available.

Premium is not available to purchase yet.

This page is informational only. There is no checkout, payment form, subscription signup or purchase link on this website. Availability, included features and final prices will be announced before subscriptions open.

What is Premium?

More options. One training platform.

Premium is being planned as an optional paid tier for UltimateTrainer. The exact feature list is still being finalized, so we are not advertising features that have not been confirmed or released.

+

Optional upgrade

Premium is intended to add optional capabilities without making a paid subscription a requirement to use the standard UltimateTrainer experience.

Designed for the app

Premium subscription

Simple weekly or monthly billing.

Premium will have two subscription options: $1.99 weekly or $4.99 monthly. Both plans automatically renew until cancelled. The monthly plan saves 37% compared with four weekly payments. These details are shown for information only; purchases are not available through this website.

Automatic renewal and cancellation

Both the $1.99 weekly and $4.99 monthly subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each billing period. You can cancel at any time to stop future renewals. Your Premium access remains active until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Subscription time

Under the planned model, subscription time continues to run during the full paid period, whether or not the UltimateTrainer application is open.

Common questions

Premium FAQ.

Can I buy Premium on this website?

No. This website does not accept payments or create subscriptions. It only provides information. Purchase and activation details, if and when Premium launches, will be announced separately.

What are the subscription prices?

The planned prices are $1.99 every 7 days or $4.99 per month. The monthly option saves 37% compared with paying for four weekly periods.

What features are included?

The Premium feature lineup has not been finalized. Confirmed features and any limitations will be listed here before launch.

Will UltimateTrainer still have a standard version?

Yes. Premium is planned as an optional upgrade, not a replacement for the standard UltimateTrainer experience.

How does cancellation work?

Canceling stops the next automatic renewal. You keep Premium access through the end of the billing period you already paid for.

When will Premium launch?

No launch date is being promised yet. This page will be updated when the feature set, prices and activation process are ready.

Questions about Premium?

For questions or feedback about the planned subscription, contact the UltimateTrainer team. Please note that contacting us does not reserve a subscription or initiate a purchase.
