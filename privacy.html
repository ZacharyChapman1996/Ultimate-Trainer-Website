Effective date: November 27, 2026
This notice explains what information may be handled when you visit the UltimateTrainer website, email us, download our software or use one of our applications. Different applications may handle information in different ways, so this page focuses on the website and our general communications.

If you email us, we receive the details you choose to include, such as your email address, message and any attachments. We use those details to respond to you or help with the issue you contacted us about.

2. Cookies and analytics

We do not currently run our own analytics or advertising-tracking system on this website.

3. How we use information

Information you send us may be used to answer questions, provide support, investigate bug reports and deal with security or misuse issues.

4. When information is shared

We do not maintain a separate database of personal information collected through this website, and we do not sell personal information. 5. Retention and security

Emails and related information are kept for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide support, maintain relevant records or address security issues. We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control, but email and internet services cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices

If you have sent us personal information, you can contact us with questions or ask us to correct or delete it. We will review your request and handle it where applicable. Some information may need to be kept for operational, security or legal reasons.

7. Changes to this notice

If our website or the way we handle information changes, we may update this page. The effective date at the top will show when the latest version took effect.
