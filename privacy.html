Privacy Notice

Effective date: November 27, 2026
This notice explains what information may be handled when you visit the UltimateTrainer website, email us, download our software or use one of our applications. Different applications may handle information in different ways, so this page focuses on the website and our general communications.

1. Information you send us

If you email us, we receive the details you choose to include. This may include your email address, the contents of your message, attachments and any information needed to look into your question or issue.

2. Website and hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When someone visits, GitHub and the providers that support its service may process technical details such as an IP address, browser information, the time of the request and the page being requested. GitHub's own privacy terms apply to information it handles.

We do not run a website account or registration system, and the website does not take payments. The Premium page is there to explain the planned subscription; it does not offer checkout, collect payment details or create subscriptions.

Some parts of the site, such as fonts, may load from other providers. Those requests are handled under the relevant provider's privacy practices.

3. Cookies and analytics

We do not currently run our own analytics or advertising-tracking system on this website. GitHub or other services used to deliver parts of the site may use their own technologies. If we add analytics or tracking ourselves, we will update this notice.

4. Our applications

UltimateTrainer also develops desktop software, gaming tools and plugins. What an application handles depends on what it does. Some features may work entirely on your computer; others may connect to online services or third-party platforms.

When an application uses information in a way that needs its own explanation, that information should be covered in the application's documentation or a separate notice. This website notice is not a full description of every application.

5. Downloads and other websites

Downloads and project files may be hosted by services such as GitHub. If you follow a link or download a file from another service, that provider's privacy policy applies to the information it collects. We do not control how external services handle data.

6. How we use information

We use information you send us to respond to messages, help with support requests, investigate bug reports, communicate about our projects and deal with security or misuse issues. We do not use it for purposes unrelated to the reason it was provided unless we have a valid reason to do so.

7. When information is shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may pass through providers that help us host the website, handle email or distribute software. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our users, projects or services.

8. Retention and security

We keep messages and related information for as long as we reasonably need them to respond, provide support, keep relevant records or address security issues. We take reasonable steps to protect information we control, but email and internet services cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your choices

If you have sent us personal information, you can contact us with questions or ask us to correct or delete it. We will review the request and handle it where applicable. We may need to keep some information for operational, security or legal reasons. Privacy rights can also depend on where you live.

10. Changes to this notice

If our website or the way we handle information changes, we may update this page. The effective date at the top will show when the latest version took effect.
